About Me

Full Stack Developer & GIS/BI Engineer

I am FALINIRINA ANDRIANJAFY Christian, a Business Intelligence and Database Development Engineer with extensive experience in Full Stack Web, Mobile, and GIS development. For over two decades, I have been working as a consultant and software engineer in Madagascar and France, designing and developing integrated systems, data warehouses, and web-mapping platforms.
